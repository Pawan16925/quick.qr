QuickQR Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

1. Introduction

Welcome to QuickQR. This Privacy Policy explains how QuickQR handles information when you use our application.

By using QuickQR, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

QuickQR is designed to work with minimal personal information. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, home address, or password.

Important: QR codes may contain information that you enter yourself. Please do not put sensitive or confidential information into a QR code unless you understand the risks.

3. Camera Permission

QuickQR may request access to your device camera when you use the QR code scanning feature.

Camera access is used only to scan QR codes. QuickQR does not use the camera to identify you or record you for unrelated purposes.

4. QR Code Data

QR code content may include text, links, contact information, Wi-Fi information, or other data depending on what you scan or create.

QR code data is processed only to provide the requested application functionality.

5. Data Storage

If QuickQR stores QR codes or related information on your device, that information remains on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

QuickQR does not intentionally upload your QR code content to our own servers unless clearly stated in the application.

6. Sharing

QuickQR may provide Android's standard sharing functionality so you can share generated QR codes or their content with other applications.

When you use the sharing feature, the information is handled according to the privacy policy of the application or service you choose to share with.

7. Third-Party Services

QuickQR may use third-party Android or Google services required for application functionality, security, distribution, or crash reporting.

Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

QuickQR does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
